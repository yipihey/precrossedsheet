WebGPU numerical checks

The direct-mode suite compares continuous sheet Fourier modes with independent f64 quadrature. The resident suite checks FFT roundtrips, periodic PM forces and JVPs, matched Rust PM trajectories, and full-run provenance. Finite-difference checks compare GPU centered differences with an independent analytic fixture and the retained Rust f64 request oracle. Add ?fd=1 or ?fd=1&fd-benchmark=1 for its numerical suite and matched timings; ?fd-repeat=1 runs 20 fixed-input repeats each for 3D n=32 waves and Gaussian LCDM, recording raw outputs, delta amplitudes, and SHA-256 hashes. Other options include ?resident=1, ?resident-worker=1, and ?resident=1&resident-benchmark=1.

Direct-mode and isolated force comparison target: each primal and JVP component is assessed against absolute error 10⁻⁶ plus relative error 10⁻⁴ on f32-quantized inputs. The full-request amplitude derivative still requires the existing relative error at most 10⁻⁵. Resident trajectories have separate component budgets shown in the report; see the PM implementation and precision contract.
